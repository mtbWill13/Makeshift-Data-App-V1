Your events

These are the events your team scouts. Each one has a name, an event key and (when you're ready) the Google Sheet for it. They're the choices in every page's event dropdown.

    Add an event, or update one

    The key is the event's code on thebluealliance.com (the year, then letters). It's how public data like rankings is found. For a practice event, make one up, like 2026practice.

    Make a copy of the team template sheet, then share your copy with … as an Editor. To connect a sheet to an event you already added, enter the same key again with the link.

    Team password

    Changing the team password logs everyone else out.

    Delete this account

    This removes your team's login, colours and event list from this site, and everyone on your team is logged out. Your Google Sheets are not deleted; they stay in your Google account. Your team number can be signed up again afterwards.
